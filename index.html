Frequently asked questions

Does Remi require an account?
No. Remi does not ask for an account, identity or personal profile information.

Why does Remi ask for location access?
Only to show the weather card. An approximate (rounded) version of your location is sent to the Open-Meteo service. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where does the weather data come from?
